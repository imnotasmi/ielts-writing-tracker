How It Works

Get up and running in less than 3 minutes.

01

Install & Setup

Open any Google Sheet, launch the add-on from the menu, and click Setup Sheets to generate all pre-formatted tabs.

02

Add Your Key

Paste your free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. It is securely saved to your Google User Properties.

03

Brainstorm & Write

Generate ideas or write your essay (~250 words) and hit Analyse Essay in the sidebar for immediate examiner feedback.

04

Track & Rewrite

Review your rubric scores, generate higher-band model rewrites, and watch your progress charts update automatically.

Privacy First: Bring Your Own API Key (BYOK)

Your essays never pass through our servers. Your Google Gemini API key is stored locally inside your Google account's Apps Script User Properties. All prompts and evaluations flow directly between your sheet and Google's Gemini API.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your key is saved using Google Apps Script's UserProperties. This means it is tied only to your personal Google account. It is never stored in spreadsheet cells, and cannot be viewed by other users even if you share your sheet.
Google AI Studio offers a generous free tier for Gemini models (such as Gemini Flash). For personal IELTS practice, most students never exceed the free tier limits. If you choose a paid plan, analyzing an essay costs less than a fraction of a cent.
No AI tool can guarantee official IELTS results. The add-on uses an AI examiner persona strictly calibrated against the official Task 2 public band descriptors (Task Response, Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammar) to guide practice and identify gaps.
The Analytics and Dashboard sheets require at least one successfully analyzed essay in your Practice Log to compute trends and render the charts.
